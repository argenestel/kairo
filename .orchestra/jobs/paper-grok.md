## Deliverable 1 — paper/cross-chain-options.md (the paper; ~5,000–8,000 words, rigorous but readable)
Title and name the mechanism yourself (short, memorable). Must contain:
1. Abstract; the problem (options/prediction liquidity siloed per chain; each venue has its own oracle and its own notion of "the price at expiry"; bridging collateral adds credit risk).
2. The binary atom (price = probability, max loss = premium, 100% collateral, no margin/liquidations) and outcome-first UX.
3. **Canonical events:** a market is identified chain-agnostically by (asset, strike, expiry, comparator); the same key on every chain. Formalise.
4. **Oracle-equivalent settlement:** "first print at or after expiry", and how each oracle class proves/approximates it onchain: Chainlink (round adjacency proof with a round-id hint — exact), Pyth (prev_publish_time < T ≤ publish_time — exact when posted; sponsored-feed window otherwise), HyperCore precompile (recorded inside a short window — first recorder chooses within the window). Define the equivalence band between venues (expected |P_A − P_B| from oracle construction + timing), what happens when venues disagree near the strike (basis risk), and voids.
5. **Collateral-local, liquidity-global:** every venue is fully collateralised in local USDC; nothing cross-chain is ever under-collateralised. Cross-venue routing of tickets (best venue), one model-priced maker quoting the same fair value on every venue, cross-venue inventory netting.
6. **Contingent-surplus netting (the new primitive — develop it carefully):** on a venue, YES+NO pairs are backed by $1 each. Burning a YES on venue A (without its NO) leaves venue A over-collateralised by exactly $1 *if YES wins*; burning a NO on venue B leaves B over-collateralised by $1 *if NO wins*. Hence YES_A + NO_B together are worth exactly $1 in every state, payable after settlement from whichever venue holds the surplus (message proof + CCTP-style USDC transfer). Build on this: (a) cross-chain merge, (b) position "teleport" (burn on A, mint on B against a contingent-surplus receipt settled post-expiry), (c) maker netting. State invariants and prove them (simple algebra is fine), the trust assumptions of the messaging layer, failure modes (venues disagree on the outcome! → define the rule: each venue's surplus is paid per its own oracle; quantify the residual basis risk and who bears it; a "disagreement reserve"), latency, and what can be done before vs after expiry.
7. UX: one ticket ("I think SOL will be above $120 by Friday with $100"), venue routing, wallets per chain, settle-anywhere payouts.
8. Security & threat model (oracle manipulation per class, settlement-window games, messaging-layer compromise, griefing, MEV on routing), economics (fees, maker risk caps).
9. **Implementation status — be scrupulously honest:** LIVE on testnets: per-venue CLOB + collateral + settlement (Solana devnet program MonoTs1KRuamxFReiqsMzVt7gFM8bbn3QmEFjMbyazh with Pyth; Base Sepolia with Chainlink once deployed; Arbitrum Sepolia + HyperEVM in progress), same canonical keys across chains, multi-chain UI with routing. PROPOSED / not built: contingent-surplus netting, teleport, cross-venue maker netting. Table.
10. Roadmap, related work, references.
